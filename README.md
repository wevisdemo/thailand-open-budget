### Project Description

Thailand’s public expenditure budget data is currently published across more than 30 budget documents, making it difficult to access, reuse, and navigate—and ultimately limiting the public’s ability to scrutinize how public money is spent.

We aim to address this challenge by developing an open budget platform that makes it easier for people to search, track, and question government spending.

### Goal

Since 2022, we have been converting draft budget data from PDF & non-machinable Excel documents into CSV datasets and developing Open Budget data visualizations designed primarily for year-to-year analysis. (THAILAND BUDGET 2566, 2567, 2568, 2569) The goal of this project is, therefore, to bring budget data from different years together in one place, expand the coverage and completeness of the budget database, and make the data easier to explore and use.
